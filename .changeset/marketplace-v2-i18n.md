The marketplace v2 plugin is translated. Every visible string of the Explore and Library tabs, the Try dialog, the detail view, the theme cards and the trial banner now comes from the new `marketplace` namespace, shipped in English and German next to the other core namespaces; the sidebar entry resolves its title through the same key. Diagnostic messages from the Try security gates (allowlist, protocol, integrity) stay English: they quote configuration keys for administrators.
