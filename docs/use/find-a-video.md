# Find a video

For everyone who needs one specific recording. Afterwards you'll be able to search, sort, choose columns, and page through the video table.

Open **Videos** in the left navigation. The list shows every recording you may work with, newest first.

## Search

Type into **Search…**. The list reloads about half a second after you stop typing and jumps back to page 1. The search runs on the server, so it looks at *all* your recordings — not only the page in front of you.

**Reset**, beside the search box, appears while a search is active and clears it.

## Sort

Click a column header. Sortable headers carry a small arrow and open a menu:

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ascending** | A→Z, earliest first |
| **Descending** | Z→A, latest first |
| **None** | back to the default order |
| **Hide** | removes that column from the table |

Sorting also runs on the server, so it orders every recording rather than shuffling the current page. Only some columns offer it — **Title**, **Series**, **Status**, **Presenter**, **Origin**, and **Date**. Headers without an arrow have no menu. The table starts sorted by **Date**, latest first.

## Choose columns

**View** opens **Show/Hide Columns**, one checkbox per column. The menu stays open so you can toggle several in a row, and your choice is remembered in this browser for your next visit. *Hide* in a column's own menu does the same for that one column.

## Page through the list

**Rows per page** under the table offers 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50. Beside it, **Page 1 of 3** and four arrows move you first / back / forward / last. Changing the page size returns you to page 1.

## List or gallery

If your deployment enabled it, a grid button left of *View* swaps the table for a gallery of thumbnails showing title, series, presenter, date, and location. Search, sorting, and paging work the same. The choice is not remembered — a reload puts you back in the list.

## The videos of one series

The video count on the [Series](./series.md) screen opens this same table limited to that series, and the heading then reads **Videos /** followed by the series title. That heading is the only sign a filter is active, and there is no button to clear it: click **Videos** in the left navigation to see everything again.

## When nothing shows up

- **No results found** while a search is active — the search matched nothing. Clear it with *Reset*.
- **There are currently no videos available.** — you have no recordings at all. The upload symbol beneath it leads to [the upload page](./upload.md).
